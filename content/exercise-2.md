# Exercise 2: Wiki Introduction

Write the introduction to a wiki article about something in your job that people should understand.

### Choose a topic

Choose something bounded and familiar — not your entire team’s architecture.

- Engineers: an application, major system, or important data pipeline.
- Non-engineers: a recurring team process or major initiative.

### Your audience

Someone who joined your team a few weeks ago. They understand the general lay of the land, but they do not yet know this topic.

### Your introduction should answer

- What is this thing?
- Why should I care?
